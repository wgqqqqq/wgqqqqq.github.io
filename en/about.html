I am Guanqi, an independent engineer focused on agentic software, developer tools, and local-first interfaces.

I care about software that keeps its mechanics visible. Sessions, tools, state, logs, and runtime boundaries should be inspectable instead of hidden behind an interface that only looks smooth.

This site is now a blog first. I use it to write down problems, design judgments, and unfinished thoughts from building software. The homepage points to recent notes; this page keeps the short bio and public links.
